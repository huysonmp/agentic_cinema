### Region-specific comparison at the actual integrated scale

The location labels below refer to visible image regions; no image coordinates, crop measurements or pixel-identity test were performed.

1. **Central food mound, both targets:** colour and loose piled presentation broadly transfer from F05. Some pink-tan flatter fragments remain, especially toward the front/lower-right of the mound; this is not an assertion that every piece became a round strand. However, the upper surface and centre are dominated by many short, plump, rounded curls of similar visual weight. At the table-image scale, widened flat faces, thin ribbon edges and the slice-versus-strip variation prominent in F05 and `nembui.jpg` are substantially less legible. The second documentary photo also has curls, so curvature itself is not the defect; the defect is the integrated balance/readability of flat-versus-rounded forms.
2. **Leaf lining around the central plate, both targets:** elongated green blades with visible midribs/lateral veins and pointed ends broadly resemble the broad-leaf silhouettes in `nembui.jpg`. They are not the lobed silhouette of some other fig species, and the inspected VNUF standard does not require lobes. These observations support visual compatibility only. No unique sung-species identification is made, and the lining's inclusion remains an owner design choice.
3. **Broad-leaf stack on rear-left plate, both targets:** several whole broad leaves can be distinguished from the finer sprigs. Smooth margins and central/lateral vein patterns are readable. The uppermost visible leaf is relatively rounded in this perspective, with a less pronounced extended tip than some leaves in the documentary reference. That difference is not enough to assert a wrong species: foreshortening, stacking and stylisation remain plausible. Broad/simple-leaf grouping is evidenced; species correctness remains unverified.
4. **Fine sprigs on rear-left plate, both targets:** branching stems and narrow pointed leaf forms are visible, so the intended contrast with the broad stack survives integration. At this scale the cluster remains dense, and repeated small leaf forms look comparatively smooth/simple. Multi-order compound attachment and toothed/lobed leaflet edges are not sufficiently readable to verify the NParks diagnostic traits. This is a verification/readability gap; it is not proof that the image contains another named herb. T-NB-03 retains the cluster, although the characters attract more attention.
5. **Serving grouping, both targets:** food, a separate accompaniment plate and red sauce saucers occupy the same table scene. No surrounding shrimp, sausage or fried dish from the documentary tray photo has appeared. One can read the major groups without a food-only cutaway. Still images cannot establish the diners' ability to reach, fold, dip, transfer or eat these items. Those movement/occlusion checks are routed to staging/motion review, not claimed by this report.
