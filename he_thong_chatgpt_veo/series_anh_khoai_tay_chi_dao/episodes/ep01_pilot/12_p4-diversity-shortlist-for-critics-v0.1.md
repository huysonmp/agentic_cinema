## Grouped variants retained, not deleted

- **SEN-02 + J3:** image/caption editing is substantially one mechanism; useful fallback if B's camera move fails, but more exposition risk.
- **KV2 + J1:** fill missing information; KV2's compact tile might be more efficient than J1's map.
- **SEN-03 + KV3:** shift the question together; SEN-03 puts the dish first and may be clearer than D's card swap.
- **SEN-01 + J2:** both may show gạo rang→thính, yet A is a visual causal trace while E is sound-led inference and correction. Critics should decide whether this is meaningful diversity or superficial difference.

## Common boundary for critics

Nem Bùi should be identifiable early; Bùi Xá, Bắc Ninh is only a **gắn với** association [F01]; roasted-rice thính **góp phần** to sensory properties [F02]. No fictional product provenance, unique recipe, sniff-to-certainty, history age, generalized hospitality, or approved asset claim. All process graphics are illustrative until verified in P6. Ask whether any candidate has enough embodied food-centered action; if five are too static, recommend a targeted new exploration, not an artificial shortlist.
