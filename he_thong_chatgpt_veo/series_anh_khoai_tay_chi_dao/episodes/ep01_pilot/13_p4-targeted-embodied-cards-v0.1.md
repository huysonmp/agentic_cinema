## X1 — Miếng cuối chưa thuộc về ai

**Mechanism:** a small contest over the last piece turns into a choice to share, not a taste verdict. **Hook:** Đào and Khoai both reach toward the last piece of Nem Bùi already in frame. **Beats:** Đào offers it to the selective eater; Khoai pauses the move because he does not want the act of tasting to become a general judgment; they ask what makes this dish worth noticing beyond “ngon không”, then Khoai shares the piece. **Agency:** Đào changes invitation from taste verdict to inquiry; Khoai chooses sharing and bounded information. **F01/F02:** place association can appear early in neutral text/line; F02 can be mentioned only as sourced detail, not inferred from chewing/smell. **Payoff:** from contesting one bite to sharing curiosity. **Visual:** reach → hold → divide. **Risk:** F02 is currently explanatory, not causally integrated; cut/divide and food form need fidelity review; risk of a generic “sharing food” sketch.

## X2 — Món biến mất trước bài giới thiệu

**Mechanism:** the dish visibly dwindles while Khoai prepares an over-complete introduction, forcing a change of behavior. **Hook:** Nem Bùi and neutral Bùi Xá association appear immediately; Đào starts eating as Khoai begins explaining. **Beats:** the plate empties while Khoai cuts his prepared introduction short; he asks what is worth carrying forward and Đào asks about thính; he answers F02 conservatively. **Agency:** Khoai drops lecture mode, Đào stops to ask a substantive question rather than offer a taste verdict. **Payoff:** both exchange their habitual rhythm, with a final piece still on the plate. **Visual:** dwindling dish → interrupted prepared speech → changed hands/attention. **Risk:** may caricature Đào as overeater/Khoai as bore; F02 still arrives by exposition; eating, chopsticks, continuity and lip-sync could be difficult in Veo.
