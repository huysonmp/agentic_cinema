## Decision boundaries for the media review

1. Cold description uses shapes/materials/layout actually visible, without naming leaves from filename, intended prompt, or caption.
2. After cold notes are fixed, compare actual references and source morphology. Species stays `UNKNOWN` if compound structure/margins/venation are too small, occluded, stylized or ambiguous.
3. A serving set containing compatible nem thính, sung and/or đinh lăng, and an appropriate sauce may be a valid visual interpretation. “Duy nhất”, “bắt buộc đủ cả hai loại lá” or “chỉ tương ớt” are unsupported by FOOD-01.
4. Still image cannot prove ingredient composition, taste, correct preparation, species authenticity, or handling sequence. Food resemblance and visual fidelity are narrower claims.
5. Rework findings must name actual candidate region and evidence; uncertain leaf identity is a hold dependency, not invented certainty. Owner retains asset selection and P6 gate.
