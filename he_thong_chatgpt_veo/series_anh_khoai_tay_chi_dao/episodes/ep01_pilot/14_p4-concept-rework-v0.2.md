## B/KV1-v0.2 — Điều khung hình không tự chứng minh

**Mechanism:** shifting the camera away from a potential reviewer toward Nem Bùi reveals a second problem: close-up beauty alone cannot establish how thính contributes to the dish. This limitation causes both characters to change the information design, not to fake visible evidence.

1. **Opening:** Nem Bùi is already in the frame; neutral text states it is *gắn với Bùi Xá, Bắc Ninh* [F01]. Khoai prepares to deliver a careful commentary; Đào redirects the phone so food, not his face, is central.
2. **Turn:** Khoai moves toward a close-up as if to point to a source of flavor, then **stops himself**: this image cannot prove whether a specific visible surface shows thính. Đào keeps the real dish in view and adds a separate, explicitly illustrative and research-based cue “gạo rang → thính” [F02]; no powder is fabricated onto the dish. Any final line must say at most that roasted-rice thính contributes to aroma/taste in described methods, not claim deduction by sight or smell.
3. **Payoff:** Khoai voluntarily steps out of center; the illustration recedes and both look again at the dish with a new, bounded question about what one image cannot show. No taste verdict.

**Agency:** Đào changes the frame and chooses how to signal researched information; Khoai catches his own evidentiary overreach and changes his action. **Risks:** self-reflexive media talk may displace the dish; graphic/food visuals need P6 research and asset checks; source cue must not be treated as proof about the exact fictional plate. Concept-level appeal and 30s feasibility remain untested.

## X2-v0.2 — Giới thiệu ngắn trước miếng cuối

**Mechanism:** the plate shrinks as **both** characters take portions, so Khoai shortens an over-complete introduction and Đào stops at a final piece; neither is caricatured as overeater or lecturer.

1. Nem Bùi and Bùi Xá association [F01] appear at opening; Khoai prepares a long introduction, Đào invites him to take a portion with her.
2. Both move pieces from plate; Khoai discards unverified adjectives/verdicts, Đào pauses at the last piece instead of treating it as an eating contest.
3. They keep the last piece and choose a sourced detail to carry forward: thính gạo rang góp phần tạo mùi/vị [F02]; they end curious, not judging.

**Positive change:** more mutual agency and less caricature. **Unresolved defect — `NOT_SOLVED`:** F02 is still a fact chosen after the physical action, not the discovery that causes it. The concept could work with another food unchanged. It does not yet fulfill P3 D1=A strongly enough for owner shortlist. No visual crumbs or reaction to tasting may be used as proof of thính. Eating/continuity/fidelity remain untested.
