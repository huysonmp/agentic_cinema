## Lens 1 — sensory / visible action

### SEN-01 — Hạt gạo mở đường
**Mechanism:** forward match-cut trace, from a rice-roasting illustration through thính to the dish. **Hook:** what does a roasted grain have to do with Nem Bùi? **Beats:** Nem Bùi appears early with Bùi Xá association [F01]; short illustrative gạo rang → thính sequence [F02]; return to dish with a new detail to explore. **Agency:** Khoai protects the claim boundary, Đào redirects attention from whole dish to its less obvious detail. **Payoff:** curiosity about the dish, not taste verdict. **Risk:** becomes a cooking tutorial or implies one mandatory method; food and process visuals require P6 fidelity. Smell cannot travel through video.

### SEN-02 — Khung hình thiếu một câu
**Mechanism:** revise a dish image/card rather than show preparation. **Hook:** a pretty dish picture may fail to tell viewers what to remember. **Beats:** Đào frames Nem Bùi and place [F01]; Khoai asks for a substantive detail; together they add a clearly illustrative thính cue [F02]. **Agency:** Đào solves by visual design; Khoai protects precision and accepts that the first image was not false, only incomplete. **Payoff:** dish image plus an accurate layer. **Risk:** meta-production conversation can displace the dish; never imply thính always visible on the outside.

### SEN-03 — Hai cách chú ý
**Mechanism:** one tableau, two observations, then a change in the question they ask. **Hook:** what do Khoai and Đào notice first in the same Nem Bùi frame? **Beats:** dish and Bùi Xá association named [F01]; they bring an illustrative source card for thính gạo rang [F02]; invitation shifts from immediate taste verdict to looking more closely. **Agency:** Đào changes her invitation, Khoai collaborates instead of judging. **Payoff:** curiosity about a layer of the dish. **Risk:** static explanatory dialogue; silent storyboard test needed.

## Lens 2 — two methods / character choices

### KV1 — Lệch tâm máy quay
**Mechanism:** physically turn the camera from Khoai-as-reviewer to the dish. **Hook:** why does Đào move Khoai out of the center? **Beats:** Khoai prepares a careful comment, Đào centers the nem; their next question uncovers thính [F02]; Đào adds Nem Bùi–Bùi Xá [F01] and Khoai willingly moves closer to the dish. **Agency:** Đào changes the viewpoint, Khoai moves from guarding a verdict to helping the dish be seen. **Payoff:** the food becomes center without a verdict. **Risk:** meta filming can take too long; close food visuals must be faithful.

### KV2 — Mảnh ghép còn thiếu
**Mechanism:** fill a missing information tile in a visual puzzle. **Hook:** dish and place are labeled, but what makes them memorable? **Beats:** Đào leaves a blank tile; Khoai rejects an unsupported taste adjective; sourced thính detail [F02] becomes a small illustrative tile beside Nem Bùi/Bùi Xá [F01]. **Agency:** both negotiate what a compelling but accurate memory cue is. **Payoff:** three pieces form a recall mark. **Risk:** too much text/infographic in 30 seconds; graphic must not imply exclusive ingredient.

### KV3 — Đổi câu hỏi
**Mechanism:** exchange who holds the “what we know” and “what we wonder” cards. **Hook:** two characters choose different questions before the same dish. **Beats:** Khoai states F01 conservatively; Đào brings documented thính [F02] to curiosity; they swap cards so Khoai asks an open question rather than deciding taste. **Agency:** Đào does factual discovery, Khoai becomes the curious one without changing personality. **Payoff:** curiosity from a character shift. **Risk:** abstract/expository; little surface action unless storyboard finds a visual event.

## Lens 3 — journey of one detail, not fictional product provenance

### J1 — Điểm dừng trên bản đồ món ăn
**Mechanism:** fill a blank “memory point” on their living food map. **Hook:** among many nem dishes, what makes this one stick? **Beats:** Đào adds Nem Bùi; Bùi Xá association [F01] is placed at the proper level; roasted-rice thính detail [F02] fills the blank. **Agency:** Đào makes the map inviting, Khoai keeps evidence exact; each accepts a part of the other's method. **Payoff:** completed map opens a further question. **Risk:** static infographic and excessive labeling; does not depict a real shipment from Bùi Xá.

### J2 — Một tiếng rang, hai cách nghe
**Mechanism:** a sound clue is resolved by a visual explanatory reveal. **Hook:** what could a brief roasting sound have to do with Nem Bùi? **Beats:** sound prompts Đào's question, Khoai refuses to infer from sound alone; illustrative gạo rang/thính appears [F02]; dish and Bùi Xá association appear [F01]. **Agency:** Đào supplies curiosity, Khoai moves from skepticism to a bounded explanation. **Payoff:** the sound becomes a memory cue, not evidence. **Risk:** audio cannot prove the process; dish reveal may be too late and a cooking sequence may seem canonical.

### J3 — Chú thích nào đáng giữ?
**Mechanism:** joint editing of a dish card/caption. **Hook:** a beautiful Nem Bùi image is missing something meaningful. **Beats:** Đào chooses image; Khoai adds Bùi Xá [F01] but makes caption dry; Đào integrates thính [F02] as a shorter invitation to explore. **Agency:** Khoai softens a fact-file style, Đào keeps the sourced detail. **Payoff:** attractive image with a real memory point. **Risk:** similar to SEN-02, dialogue about making content may overtake actual food; no source image is automatically usable as asset.
